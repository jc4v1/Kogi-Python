Participant Background (5-10 minutes)
Opening:
"Thank you for participating in this validation interview. We're evaluating a compliance framework from a legal practitioner's perspective. Before we begin discussing the framework, I'd like to understand your professional background."

Professional Experience

1. Can you describe your current role and areas of legal practice?
2. How many years of experience do you have in compliance, regulatory, or technology law?
3. What types of compliance challenges do you typically encounter in your work?

Technical Familiarity

1. How would you describe your familiarity with computational or automated compliance tools?
2. Have you worked with process modeling, formal methods, or compliance engineering approaches before?

Framework Introduction (5 minutes)
Transition:
1. "Now I'll provide a brief overview of the framework we're evaluating. [Provide demonstration/walkthrough of the framework, showing key components, workflow, and sample outputs]."

Core Validation Questions (25-35 minutes)
Theme 1: Alignment with Legal Practice
Question 1: To what extent does the framework align with your current compliance analysis practices?

Follow-up probes:

1. How does this compare to your typical workflow for analyzing legal requirements?
2. What aspects feel familiar or intuitive from a legal perspective?
3. Are there gaps between what the framework offers and what you need in practice?
4. Does the framework support the way you naturally think about compliance problems?

Theme 2: Understandability and Actionability of Outputs
Question 2: Does the framework provide outputs that are understandable and actionable from a legal perspective?

Follow-up probes:

1. Can you interpret what the framework is telling you about compliance status?
2. Would the outputs help you advise clients or make compliance decisions?
3. What additional information or context would make the outputs more useful?
4. How do the visual representations (e.g., goal models) support or hinder your understanding?
5. If the framework identified non-compliance, would you know what action to take?

Theme 3: Workflow Clarity and Practicality
Question 3: Is the workflow for linking process models and high-level legal requirements clear and practical?

Follow-up probes:

1. Does the translation process from legal text to operational artifacts make sense?
2. Where in the workflow do you see the need for legal expertise or judgment?
3. Are there steps that seem ambiguous or require clarification?
4. How does the framework handle legal terms that require interpretation?
5. Would you be able to use this framework without extensive technical training?
6. What challenges do you foresee in applying this in real-world scenarios?

Theme 4: Explainability and Transparency
Question 4: Do you see potential for the framework to improve the explainability of compliance outcomes?

Follow-up probes:

1. If this framework identified non-compliance, could you explain why to a client or regulator?
2. What would you need to justify a compliance decision made using this framework?
3. Does the framework make explicit where expert judgment enters the analysis?
4. How important is traceability back to the original legal source?
5. In cases of adverse outcomes (e.g., denial of a claim), would the framework provide sufficient evidentiary basis?
6. Does the framework help identify where in a process non-compliance occurs?

Theme 5: Practical Value and Limitations
Question 5: What practical value do you see this framework providing in your work?

Follow-up probes:

1. In what types of cases or scenarios would this framework be most useful?
2. Could this help with high-volume compliance assessments?
3. Would this framework help you identify cases requiring closer legal review?
4. What are the main limitations you see from a legal standpoint?

Question 6: What additional legal sources or context does the framework need to incorporate?

Follow-up probes:

1. Is the legal text alone sufficient, or do you need case law, regulatory guidance, or administrative decisions?
2. How important are interpretive sources (e.g., regulatory commentary, enforcement actions)?
3. Does the framework need to account for jurisdictional variations?

Additional Insights (5-10 minutes)
Accountability and Authority

1. Who should be responsible for decisions made using this framework?
2. How does this framework affect questions of interpretive authority in legal practice?

Ambiguity Handling

1. How well does the framework handle ambiguous legal terms or requirements?
2. Should the framework flag ambiguities for human review?
3. Improvement Suggestions

1. What would make this framework more valuable for legal practitioners?
2. Are there features or capabilities you would add?

Overall Assessment

1. Would you use this framework in your practice?
2. Would you recommend it to other legal professionals?
3. What is your overall impression of the framework's potential?

Closing (2-3 minutes)
1. Is there anything else you'd like to share about the framework that we haven't covered?
2. Do you have any questions for us?
3. May we contact you for follow-up clarification if needed?

Thank you for your time and valuable insights.

Appendix: Interview Guide Principles
Structure:

Begin with open-ended questions, then use probes for depth
1. Allow participant to guide discussion within themes
2. Maintain flexibility to explore unexpected insights

Interviewer Approach:
Remain neutral; avoid leading questions:
1. Use active listening and reflective responses
2. Allow pauses for participant reflection
3. Take detailed notes or record (with consent)

Ethical Considerations:
Obtain informed consent:
1. Ensure anonymity/confidentiality
2. Allow participant to skip questions or withdraw
3. Provide framework documentation for reference

Data Management:
Transcribe interview verbatim:
1. Anonymize transcript (use participant codes)
2. Store securely in project repository
3. Code responses by theme for analysis
